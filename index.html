Privacy Policy

100% Offline & Private

Calcora operates entirely on your local device. None of your calculations, financial evaluations, or inputs are transmitted to external servers.

Zero Data Tracking

We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising IDs, or behavioral tracking libraries. Your usage patterns stay completely confidential.

Permissions Usage

The VIBRATE permission is used exclusively to generate subtle tactile haptic feedback when tapping keypad buttons.

Local Storage Only

Your calculation history and customized preferences are stored in your device's private app storage. You can purge all calculation history at any time from Settings.
